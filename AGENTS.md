- One row = one production point of the Government Officer dataset, as production counts them (owner 2026-11-06: “อยากได้ทั้งสี่แสนจุด”). Never merge repeated points of one office (same type and name, same province, chained within 100 m) into one row: they stay rows; only their personnel is counted once (`staffShared`). The rows and Σ officer per district × category reconcile with production's government-agency info at build time (the build stops otherwise).
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`, `rowValue`, `unitFor`): points (“แห่ง”, `count`) by default, personnel recorded (Σ `officer` of the types that record a count at the site, “คน”, `li.supply`) on the switch; the map, legend, labels, Aha, ranks and charts read the same measure. Unknown personnel is never 0: a government office with unknown personnel is counted apart, a district whose offices all have unknown personnel is `no_data` (hatch), a tile reads “—” (its title says why), and a chip, bar or row whose personnel is unknown reads in offices or “ไม่ทราบจำนวนบุคลากร”. Personnel does not apply to places (education, religion, community): never “unknown”, never a personnel bar or column, a district of places only is a measured 0. Personnel is counted once per office (the repeated points of one office) and once per local-government organisation (`staffShared`); an agency's whole workforce recorded at its head office is “ทั้งหน่วยงาน” on that office only. Never call a figure civil servants of an area, daytime population, service capacity, a density, a ranking or a performance.
- Groups follow production's categories, by stable id (`js/cues.js#GROUP_SERIES`, DATAVIZ-05 series + shape cue; no star). The default scope is every group — every production point, read as “หน่วยงานและสถานที่”, every file (`data/offices.json`, `data/groups/<group>.json`) loaded before the first paint with the launch card counting them in; `?group=offices` is the government offices (admin, police, fire, justice, military, unclassified, “หน่วยงาน”/“หน่วยงานรัฐ”); education, religion and community are places of the same production dataset, read as “สถานที่” without personnel; `?group=all` (0.1.0) reads as every group. Types are production's codes and labels; never re-type an office by its name.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the `city` preset framing up to three เขต/อำเภอ with the most of the measure in the province that holds the most of it at district level, focused automatically. Show the share of the province's whole total, numerator and denominator, the rail comparison as two bars (both bases in each bar's title and accessible text; hidden when nothing in the province is near an operating station) and the district(s) with the most outside that province. The subject follows the scope (หน่วยงานและสถานที่ · หน่วยงานรัฐ · a group · a type · บุคลากรที่บันทึก, `unitFor`). Never cut through a tie, never focus every district that holds the measure (`js/area-insight.js`). Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Membership is the production district of the point's overlay subdistrict (`row.admin = 'overlay'`). Never re-assign by point-in-polygon on display coordinates or by a name at runtime.
- Points are production's points, never an entrance or a land parcel.
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations (every operating station drawn as a quiet clickable ring; under construction named in words only, the construction pictogram and “กำลังสร้าง”). Every distance is a straight line between reference points — the distance pictogram, “ระยะตรง” in titles — never a walking route, a travel time, a jurisdiction or a catchment.
- Nearest services (`js/profile-model.js#SERVICES`): ที่ว่าการอำเภอ · สำนักงานท้องถิ่น · สถานีตำรวจ · สถานีดับเพลิง · ศาล, the nearest office of those types within 30 km straight line, only for the services production types in the point's province (Bangkok: police and fire only). Offices round an office: same group within 2 km, nearest first, with everything round it beside. Neither is a jurisdiction, a service area or a ranking.
- Recent moves and openings (`06_RECENT_PROD_GAPS`): dated, with what the date means and the agency's own announcement (public https only); “จุดเดิมใน CityMETER” only where the sheet names the production row. They show with every group or the government offices, without a type or search filter. Map links and coordinate sources are never published.
- National scale: `data/area-index.json` has every district without geometry; outlines load per province (`js/frames.js`) for the selection and for the provinces under the map at district zoom (≤ 6 per move). Never load all 77 frame files up front.
- Focus + quiet context: detail (fills, coloured cue symbols, rounded focus massing) only for the focused part. Everything else stays visible but quiet. Below zoom 8 the province frames carry the reading — except with a จังหวัด filter.
- Symbol size (Hotel rule of 2026-11-01, point 4): the symbol's area grows with the recorded personnel (`js/model.js#pinRadius`, `PIN_SCALE`, cap `STAFF_SIZE_CAP`), the colour stays the group's; an office with unknown personnel is an open (hollow) shape at the 40-person size, a place of the other groups is filled at that size; quiet context dots follow the same rule at about half size; larger symbols are drawn first. The legend key shows 50 · 250 · 1,000 คน and the hollow mark (offices only).
- Names near the middle of the view (`js/labels.js`): from zoom 12.5, focus rows in the central ellipse, ranked by `row.importance` (the type's tier, then the national percentile of recorded personnel; a missing value is never guessed), at most 9 on desktop and 5 on a phone; a label is the group cue, one short name in the form Thai readers use (อบต. · ทต. · ทม. · ทน. · สภ. · สน. · อำเภอ …) and the personnel pictogram with the count; hover or focus opens a card of icon chips. No logos.
- Terrain (`js/shell/terrain.js`) and the building view (`js/shell/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/shell/venue-xray.js`, `js/shell/maplibre-guard.js`, MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`) follow the Hotel 0.3.x contracts unchanged: choosing an office with a point switches the map to 3D and opens the two-point camera on the building `js/office-building.js#chooseBuilding` picks among the drawn buildings within 300 m of the pin (800 m once; the reach of the office's own name on the map): an OpenStreetMap point of interest named as the office (from the same OpenFreeMap tiles) decides; otherwise the pin, the distance, the side of the street and, for the types with a footprint floor (`TYPE_FOOTPRINT_M2`), the size and the setback, with the weights fitted on the buildings OpenStreetMap names for the offices (docs/BUILDING_CHOICE.md §3). A camera choice only, never a join, a count or a located-in claim. Do not reorder the massing, x-ray, station and pin layers casually.
- Buildings per area: the units of the CityMETER · Hotel build (`data/building-units/`, copied by `scripts/adapt-building-units.py` from a named Hotel commit without the per-unit hotel counts); the source per unit is decided at build time from counts recorded in the file, never at runtime. - Office sites (`docs/SITES.md`; owner 2026-11-06 “อนุมัติ ทุกข้อ”): a site groups the drawn buildings of one office's compound for display — never a deed, a parcel, a land area or ownership. Rows live in the research sheet's tab `07_SITE_BOUNDARY_CHECK`; `scripts/build-sites.py` publishes the `checked` rows to `data/sites.json` (the interior points of the buildings, their count, the date, `boundary_source`, the building source, a public layout page) and records the digest the sheet's page computes (`siteTabDigest`); the frame, the OpenStreetMap element link, the tier, the notes and the checker stay in the sheet. `boundary_source` is `osm_polygon` or `osm_polygon+site_layout`; never `osm_area` (that code means the map draws OpenStreetMap buildings there). The site groups are offered under the ODbL 1.0 and every view that shows one credits “© OpenStreetMap contributors” with its copyright link. Building points come only from `scripts/site-buildings.mjs` (the page's own map code: `site-candidates.mjs` in the container, `sites-browser.js` in the owner's browser), within 600 m of the office's point. A pin inside a frame is not a site: the name, the kind and the unit number must agree, and a neighbour's building added by mistake is worse than one missing. The map frames every building of the site with the pin (legend “อาคารในบริเวณหน่วยงาน”), the panel says “ทั้งบริเวณ N อาคาร”, Street View still faces the building `chooseBuilding` picks. Sites of the School and Temple datasets link back with `go` (`build-sites.py --external`; a layout check outranks a frame alone). The page changes only by a rebuild from the research sheet.

Every point of the scope (up to 440,005) is a quiet dot: they reach the map worker as one multipoint per look (filled, hollow) plus a point per row with recorded personnel (`js/pins.js#contextCollection`), after the first idle on the first draw. Scope-wide figures (Aha, overview, district and province summaries) are computed once per scope array and measure (`js/areas.js` cache, `overviewOf`), never per selection.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › เขต/อำเภอ with production codes and deep links `/v3/citymeter/{code}/D/{district}?d=governmentOfficer`. An office or a station is the panel heading, never a crumb.

## Discovery and dataset group (owner decision 2026-11-08)

- Every page carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`. The owner does not want search engines to index the demos while developers keep building on them: “ไม่ต้องการให้เสิช engine เข้ามาดูเดโม แต่อยากให้ dev นำ demo บน github ไป vibe coding ต่อได้ง่าย คือ SEO อันไหนจัดการได้จัดการไปเลย แต่ยังไม่ต้องให้ crawler เข้ามา index”. Keep the rest of the SEO metadata ready for production, do not block crawlers in a robots.txt (they must be able to read the noindex), add no sitemap, and never point `rel="canonical"` at landometer.com from a noindex page. `tests/discovery-and-group.test.js` guards this.
- The panel eyebrow `CITYMETER · LIVING` follows the dataset registry (`R01_DATASETS.dataset_group`; owner: “ใช้ตามทะเบียนทั้งหมด”). This dataset is LIVING in the registry read on 2026-11-08; change the label only when the registry changes.
